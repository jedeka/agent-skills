# Brief template for hypercognize

The protocol fixes the process. The brief fixes the content. Fill the brackets, delete lenses that do not apply, keep the constraint block and the closing lines.

## Full brief (a real problem or a project)

I want to understand, ab initio and out to the frontier, [what X actually is / why Y holds / whether Z is true]. Treat [the object] as the mathematical or mechanistic object it is, not as a metaphor. Build me from primitives to mastery across every lens below, then synthesize. For each lens, give the generative core I can rederive from, the exact crux, the strongest objection, and what remains open. Pair each mechanism with an intuition I can hold and the consequences that follow from it.

A. FORMAL FOUNDATIONS. Define [X] precisely. Frame it under each of [framing 1, framing 2, framing 3]. Show where the framings coincide, where they diverge, and which is deepest. State the minimal definition the rest follows from.

B. [LENS]. [The questions this lens must answer. Name the specific results, phenomena, or debates it should confront.]

C. [LENS]. [...]

[Continue one lens per letter. Put the lens that unifies the others second to last.]

[N-2]. THE UNIFIED QUESTION. Strip it to the essence. Give the strongest current candidate for a unified account, its gaps, and how close the field is.

[N-1]. HONEST FRONTIER. Separate what is solved from what is contested from what is merely unexamined. Name the open problems that matter most and the questions the field avoids, with the reason it avoids them.

[N]. SIGNAL EXTRACTION, applied throughout. Separate what replicates from what is repeated. Name the load-bearing results and people, including those who speak only through their work. Flag the overclaimed and the incentive-driven. Separate real shifts in understanding from marketing. Say what the strongest researchers are working on versus what dominates public attention.

Constraints:
- Primary sources over summaries. Frontier claims need current sources, dated.
- Retrieve every date, number, quote, citation, exact theorem statement, and present-state claim, or mark it UNKNOWN. No guessing.
- Report what you sought and did not find.
- Budget: [saturate / one capped pass of about N searches].
- Deliverable: [synthesis in chat / organized document / digest].

Fuse intuition with the exact mathematics throughout. Give me the seed, not the encyclopedia. I will verify everything.

## Short form (a sharp question)

hypercognize: [the question in one sentence]. Give me the generative core, the intuition with its breaking point, the consequences, the strongest objection, and what is open. Retrieve or mark UNKNOWN any date, number, citation, or current-state claim. [Budget: a few searches / none, mechanisms only.]

## What each element buys

- Naming lenses as separate threads steers decomposition; a single combined question returns shallow results for all of it.
- Demanding the four elements per lens builds calibration into the slots: the strongest objection and the open items cannot be filled with confident filler.
- Demanding signal extraction forces the replicates-versus-hype pass that otherwise gets skipped.
- Asking for the negative space forces the disconfirming searches into the deliverable.
- Capping the budget prevents unbounded research as the silent default.
- Naming the deliverable prevents a format guess.
