02 · Camera sets

Seven camera sets, blocked in software

camerasblockedwhat the model sees
top + left + right0everything the fixed cameras see
top + left1top view and one side view
top + right1top view and the other side view
left + right1both side views, no top view
top only2colour and position; big and small look alike from above, and one box's top carries a sticker
left only2one side view: colour and size
right only2the other side view: colour and size
none3no image: handled by rule (all four boxes, then look), not run

Blocking a camera means leaving its image out of the prompt. Every scene appears in all seven sets.

Source: 80-run-unique-scenario.md §2. The no-camera case is a rule of the decision policy, not a measured condition.
